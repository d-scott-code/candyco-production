Interpretation

What's loss vs. what's overpack vs. what's process slop

YTD chocolate variance is 146,994 lbs over BOM ($558,578). Of this, the overpack analysis above implies ~43,700 lbs of chocolate ($166,062) is sitting in finished pouches as overfill — material we didn't lose, we just gave it away to the customer. The remaining ~103,294 lbs ($392,516) is true production loss — tank residuals, tunnel cleanouts, enrober purges. Splitting the chocolate problem this way: ~28% overpack (a pricing/spec decision), ~72% process loss (a maintenance/process decision). Both are addressable, but they have different owners and different fixes.
